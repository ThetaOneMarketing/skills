# Attribution

Both skills are original implementations. Every file here was written fresh.
The *methods* they use stand on the shoulders of people who published them
first. Credit where it is due.

## raise-the-bar

Fresh-context critic, binary verdict, loop on the single biggest gap: adapted
from Matt Shumer's "gauntlet loop" pattern.

## hatch

| Stage | Method lineage | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Frame | Interview-in-rounds over a design tree ("grilling") | Matt Pocock, `mattpocock/skills` (MIT) |
| Diverge | Parallel, isolated generators under cognitive frames; generator/critic split | Udit Akhouri, `UditAkhourii/adhd` (MIT); Osborn/Parnes divergent–convergent thinking |
| Judge | Multiple independent advisors, anonymous peer review, chairman synthesis | Andrej Karpathy, `karpathy/llm-council` (MIT); five-lens adaptation popularized by Ole Lehmann |
| Red-team | Adversarial assumption audit: claim inventory, load-bearing × evidence ranking | Nate B. Jones (assumption-checking pattern); classic pre-mortem (Gary Klein) |
| Ship-gate | Fresh-context critic, binary verdict, loop on the single biggest gap | Matt Shumer ("gauntlet loop" pattern) |
